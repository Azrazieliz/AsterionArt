## Proposed taxonomy architecture — approval required before creating entries

Compare:
A. Entirely separate independently curated Theme atom ontology: excellent role-specific admission and sampling semantics but duplicates identities and descriptors.
B. Reuse Character SC leaves as the sole Theme atom universe: low initial duplication but violates breadth and leaf eligibility (a character-worthy identity is not necessarily a simple visual atom).
C. Recommended: independent typed taxonomies with a shared cross-reference registry; Theme has its own lexical atomic admission and sampling pool, can reference eligible canonical concepts from other taxonomies without importing their hierarchy or weighting. This retains distinct artistic roles and avoids duplicate identities.

The shared infrastructure should support per-taxonomy stable IDs/namespaces, recursive single canonical paths, versioned parent/child links, aliases and intersectable views, admission status, source evidence, retired IDs, explicit cross-links and non-selectable mechanism records. Never repurpose existing SC IDs. Old selections retain exact taxonomy snapshot and IDs. Distinguish shared semantic identity from methodology-specific selectable leaf membership; no implicit selection propagation across modes.

### Theme proposal (classification, not populated content)
- Object classified: an independently selectable visual-design atom, not a finished scene, bundled concept, theme, artist style or prompt. Lexical form follows recovered §25; complex combinations belong to later synthesis, not leaf admission.
- Proposed top-level dimensions for review: physical entities and structures; environments and spatial forms; materials and surfaces; geometry and organization; observable properties; motion and visual processes. A single canonical home per leaf; cross-cutting search facets can expose other applicable dimensions without duplicating sampling membership. These are candidate dimensions, not owner-approved roots.
- Leaf contract: immutable identifier; canonical path; single-word canonical lexical form; unambiguous sense; observable visual contribution; possible material/structural manifestations; compatible roles; source provenance; aliases; links to equivalent concepts elsewhere; admission/rejection reason; explicit non-selectable status for composite/method records.
- Admission tests: individually meaningful, visually usable, distinct from existing sense, not a disguised compound, not a presupposed association. Preserve breadth beyond familiar or easy-to-render ideas. Alias and cross-link do not change draw probability. Synthesis records reference original immutable atoms and authorized constraints. Random selection must remain independent of current keywords; deterministic test replay verifies that property.
- No sample leaves, new canonical IDs, probabilities, category content or training corpus approved by this proposal.
