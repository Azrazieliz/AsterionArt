# Unapproved design candidates — new taxonomies and Theme-only parameters

**STATUS: PROPOSAL / NOT OWNER-APPROVED.** This is NOT a product requirement or implementation authorization. Existing [Concepts.md baseline](../architecture/CONCEPTS_TAXONOMY_OWNER_BASELINE_20261030.md) and mode-specific methodologies prevail.

## Why the previous suggestions were inadequate

The discarded 14-category list was a **dictionary of subject areas**, not a taxonomy: it lacked canonical membership, recursive eligibility, leaf obligations, admission/rejection tests, constraint propagation and exact behavior in the app. Likewise, the discarded global 16-parameter slider set did not model actual methodology decisions. B/E/T governs **Theme Creation**, not all Originals.

## First priority: extend the existing SC concept taxonomy, without creating a second overlapping SC list

- Keep the owner-approved **BC → SF → SC** path per leaf; aliases and cross-cutting domain/query facets remain views, never second parents.
- Add research candidates through the original four dispositions, six selection tests, abstract/social heightened gate, and canonical leaf contract (§§1–4 of Concepts.md). The owner, not automatic model research, decides admission and new immutable IDs.
- Audit gaps by *missing worthwhile selectable identities*, not by producing an arbitrary quota of new categories. Introduce a new SF or BC only when current membership cannot correctly express approved leaves.
- Preserve canonical body plans, fixed counts, symbiosis requirements, examples' status, interpretation boundaries, search views and retired aliases.

**Concrete test:** an advanced term whose only value is a process or control mechanism must NOT enter the SC wildcard pool. Keep its materialization contribution in MK or T unless it passes all character-first requirements. Distinct variants under an existing leaf are not immediately new SC identities.

## Candidate new taxonomy A — Theme-source identities (first new taxonomy to consider)

**Taxonomic object:** independently selectable *atomic visual identities* eligible for creation of a complete compositional Theme, not arbitrary words, B/E/T values, materialization operations or the final approved Theme Sheet itself.

**Core classification requirements:**
- Exactly one canonical leaf path through a recursive family/branch hierarchy. Root-family titles and candidates must be researched and owner-approved; do not invent a starter roster.
- A leaf requires canonical identity, intrinsic visual structures, whole-image arrangement/interaction potential, permissible variants, material/space/subject effects, forbidden imported associations, and composition-specific fusion affordances. The leaf remains useful across multiple B/E/T and keyword settings.
- Four dispositions parallel the *function* of Concepts.md: independently selectable Theme atom; variant of an existing theme atom; non-selectable assembly/interaction operation; reject. Admission tests must be **Theme-specific**, not copied from the premium-female-character admission test.
- Admission stress test: combine the candidate with two unrelated admitted theme atoms and confirm that all three materially change one coherent whole-image system, retaining recognizable influences with an identifiable layout/interaction grammar. Reject a candidate that only contributes a color tag, unbound mood word or decorative prop.
- Explicit source links may reference an existing SC leaf, MK mechanism or T topology, but those do not become duplicate taxonomy leaves. A shared term is not automatically an SC-admitted identity and a Theme atom at the same time.
- Recursive search must resolve one chosen leaf or explicitly bounded branch, support exact aliases and cross-cutting views, and reject ambiguous compound queries rather than guessing.
- Atomic roulette samples only *admitted* leaves from the eligible selection pools and freezes their IDs before B/E/T/keywords steer textual synthesis. Separate seed, draw history and source version.

**Mode behavior:** Stage-one roulette; text-only molecule formation; alteration/fusion of approved text themes; Theme Sheet bridge. Does not apply to Character Creation's SC wildcard selection.

## Candidate new taxonomy B — Reproducible art-style grammars (second to consider)

**Taxonomic object:** reusable *rendering grammars*, not media franchises, moods, subject matter or existing characters depicted on reference images.

**Leaf contract:** canonical visual grammar defining drawing/linework, shape abstraction, shading, material rendering, color relations, finish, constraints, permissible hybridization, and how its properties persist when subjects, scenes, poses or themes change.

**Admission gate:** a style must be reproducible on unrelated source images and distinguishable by rendering treatment alone; a label such as a theme, genre, mood, palette name or character reference does not suffice. The classification must have a single canonical primary path; line/shading/medium properties are searchable facets and may overlap without creating duplicate leaves.

**Operational use:** Style Sheet assembly, Art Style Correction and Artwork style authority. On style correction, protected source identities/camera/pose/content remain protected, and style leaves affect only permitted rendering dimensions. The existing approved Art Style methodology remains the authority.

## Do not make weapon/outfit/creature/video taxonomies automatically

These can be evaluated after the owner selects specific design bottlenecks. For each, first identify the *selectable identity* and what its classification changes; otherwise it risks duplicating equipment knowledge or creating another long list of labels. Do not silently add new taxonomies when the mode already has sufficient executable configuration.

## Separate knowledge layers (reuse; no reclassification)

- SC: selectable character-concept identity.
- MK: non-selectable materialization mechanism.
- T: non-selectable topology and reciprocal-fusion structure.
- Proposed Theme atoms: selectable atomic Theme-source identity, if approved.
- Proposed style leaves: selectable rendering grammar, if approved.
- Mode methodology: execution procedure and permitted transformations, never a mere source leaf.

Taxonomy links should carry typed relationships and preservation guards, e.g. `allows-materialization`, `needs-invariant`, `applies-style-to`; they are not new canonical parenthood.

## Theme-only parameter candidates — NOT canonical

A long generic aesthetic-control list duplicates reasoning instructions and creates UI clutter. Only consider parameters whose effects can be tested as distinct operations:

1. **Fusion coupling:** how far atomic sources reciprocally transform shared geometry, materials and interactions, rather than merely appearing alongside one another. It changes the Theme synthesis operation and can be evaluated by source-removal tests.
2. **Dominance distribution:** whether each atom has equal constructional authority or whether specified atoms control macro structure while others govern subordinate systems. This is not a general aesthetic power slider; it changes the synthesis hierarchy explicitly.
3. **Five-theme separation:** an output-population criterion specifying how different the five initial text themes must be in underlying spatial/interaction structures—not just names, colors or camera. If the governing methodology already fixes that separation, do not add a redundant parameter.

B/E/T retains its original meaning with owner-expanded scope only inside Theme Creation. Its intensity must not be arbitrarily capped, and 1 should already be prominent. No new parameter label, value or default is installed solely by this proposal.

## Approval questions

1. Is the first genuinely **new** taxonomy Theme-source identities or reproducible art-style grammars? The existing SC taxonomy can be expanded independently without changing its rules.
2. Should Theme atoms admit **only intrinsic visual identities** (per the proposed gate), or can explicitly specified *organizational relationships* also be separately selectable leaves? Keeping mechanisms non-selectable as MK/T analogues is the safer clear distinction, but it requires owner decision.
3. Do any of the three Theme-only parameter candidates add real control beyond the existing atomic roulette and molecular synthesis methodology? Only accepted axes will be built; otherwise retain B/E/T and existing exact operations.

**No IDs, branch names, leaves, example atoms or new taxonomic memberships are approved or assigned here.**
