## Owner clarification — artistic intelligence must reason

The owner clarifies that specialist intelligence does not mean simplistic intelligence. The application must be capable of thinking about its own creative decisions, understanding anatomy and appropriate placement of body structures, and knowing concept combination mechanisms. Artistic intelligence is more important than general-purpose frontier capability. Learned artistic reasoning, structural/topological and spatial coherence, concept synthesis, aesthetic judgment, and anticipation of errors are required, not just concept enumeration or hard-coded checks. ## Owner clarification — aesthetics first and model communication

- Artistic preparation must prioritize exceptional aesthetics, not anatomical experimentation or structural novelty for its own sake. Concept fusion must support beautiful, coherent, distinctive character designs; anatomical and topological reasoning is an enabling competency and a correctness constraint, not the primary artistic objective. A structurally integrated fusion can still fail aesthetic acceptance. Preserve Character Creation's original preparation pass and its actual artistic requirements.
- The owner explicitly distrusts a pipeline of models communicating via generated prompts because of the previous architecture's failures. Do not reproduce cascaded free-text planning, prompt rewriting, runtime methodology instructions, or overloaded conditioning. Native model-to-model communication is a substantive engineering concern, not solved merely by calling a text output structured.
- Owner suggests one shared inference/design model specialized by the required module (potentially learned LoRA weights), followed directly by the generation model. This is a candidate architecture, not mandatory LoRA usage or a selected checkpoint. Evaluate shared artistic backbone with mode-specific trained adapters/heads versus alternatives; avoid separately loading many sequential reasoners.
- Engineering proposal for Astra to evaluate during implementation: mode-native typed design state and learned visual/semantic/spatial embeddings, mapped through trained renderer-specific conditioning projections into supported generator inputs; preserve reference pixels and identity. Native text encoding may be used where a foundation genuinely requires it, but a long generated prompt must not be the sole inter-model contract. LoRA changes model behavior, not the communication interface. Train and validate the representation-to-renderer bridge, and compare aesthetically judged actual finished outputs against a simpler compatible baseline. No claim that arbitrary hidden states can be passed directly between unrelated models. Keep any necessary critic/correction lightweight and evidence-driven, not another chain of prompt-producing models.
- Aesthetic preference and methodology fidelity must drive architecture and training selection, including comparative examples of attractive versus technically valid but aesthetically weak fusion. Existing approvals for local inference, three image families, Nexus ratings, training and automatic optimization remain unchanged. No implementation or training is claimed by this record.
