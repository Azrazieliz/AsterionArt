## Current ecosystem cross-check (engineering evidence, not further owner decisions)

- Nexus is the native merger of Core, Downloader and Rounder; TeraBox-first permanent library, shared metadata and rating, streaming/cache/pins. Core/Downloader are historical donors, not separate new runtime targets. Nexus rating is subjective liking, 0–100 per opened file, distinct from correctness Review and technical quality evaluation. Art should consume the shared canonical rating and maintain generation provenance/technical diagnostics without treating liking as an objective quality label. Source: [Nexus approved experience](https://github.com/Azrazieliz/AsterionNexus/blob/work/nexus-harmonization-20261126/docs/16-APPROVED-EXPERIENCE-AND-ORGANISATION.md).
- [Nexus ecosystem contracts](https://github.com/Azrazieliz/AsterionNexus/blob/work/nexus-harmonization-20261126/docs/08-ECOSYSTEM-INTEGRATION.md): specialist engine/project authority, signed direct actions, scoped asset refs and verified receipts; Control orchestrates complex multi-app chains. Native ordinary specialist operation must not require Control. Use bounded URI/stream access for large assets. Nexus owns canonical asset identity/provenance; Art owns image/video synthesis/project state.
- [Sound frozen master](https://github.com/Azrazieliz/Asterion-Sound/blob/main/7STAR_FROZEN_MASTER.md): Sound owns music/singing, character voices/dialogue, effects, reusable artist/voice profiles and editable audio. Art/Sound cooperate through timing, cues and lip sync; Sound does not become a video editor. Negotiate exact handoffs after current provider capabilities are verified.
- [Writer](https://github.com/Azrazieliz/Asterion-Writing): original fiction, canon, characters, scenes and chapter revisions; preserve scene/character authority and use approved asset handoffs. Its README and Nexus spec document the prospective separate Art + Sound + 3D + Writing creative-app merger; do not assume merger already exists or reimplement specialist engines.
- [3D Foundry](https://github.com/Azrazieliz/Asterion-3d): 2D-to-3D, geometry/material/rig editing and verified export. Art supplies source references and receives compatible assets, without claiming reconstructed mesh or rig acceptance.
- [NoWorld](https://github.com/Azrazieliz/NoWorld-False-Horizon): actual world and exact identity/version/import acceptance remain NoWorld authority. Art exports with provenance and does not certify generic images or GLBs as admitted world assets.
- [Control](https://github.com/Azrazieliz/Asterion-Comander): current independent general assistant/orchestration and authorized diagnostics/deployment via established authenticated private bridge. No replacement bridge. Historical Sound master Control-only/no-AI language is superseded by newer Control-specific architecture.
- These are target contracts and repository evidence, not proof of available installed endpoints or completed merger. Verify current provider versions and compatibility before implementing exact API calls.
