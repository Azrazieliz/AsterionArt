# Asterion Art reconstruction training audit

The audit covers weight updates evidenced by the clean reconstruction source and the retained training receipts. Pinned pretrained models, conversions and output generation are listed separately from weight fits. The two verified excluded bundles were moved as archives with their manifests and receipts. Native graph code and preferred checkpoint files remain available for the approved reconstruction.

The experiments verified projections and numerical graph execution. They did not establish Art Style Sheet authority, Character preparation, materialisation, complete multi-reference understanding or an Anime artist. No approved Anime artist, specialist module, receiving fine-tune, generator adaptation or video checkpoint was trained in the audited work.

Rin Flanime, MiaoMiao, Hassaku, Animagine and NoobAI evidence concerns published models and candidate comparisons. The observed MiaoMiao Q8 conversion changed representation precision. The long-context graph uses the published Anima weights and does not select a shared artist. Its text path, direct learned design input and spatial reference routing are tested as renderer capabilities.
