### Review example for Character preparation

Iris contributes three upright standards and three descending falls, with directional contrast from sword-like leaves. Porcelain contributes coherent glazed surfaces, edges, controlled thickness and the contrast between glazed and matte substrate. The preparation should produce actual body-owned construction and a compelling Anime identity.

Candidate A has a tall, quiet silhouette, three porcelain-backed upright blades born at the crown and three long curved laminae descending behind an open face. Their support continues through thin shoulders and a soft glaze-to-matte transition at the elbows. The focal color is deep indigo around the eyes and the thinnest edges. Main planes stay ivory, with restrained cobalt accents. The rear view would expose the shared crown roots and the profile would show how the falling laminae clear the neck. Candidate B concentrates the upright geometry around the shoulders and gives the head shorter blade shapes with a wider, lower silhouette. Candidate A is the review preference for its face hierarchy, recognizable downward sweep and continuous support. The pair lacks final visual acceptance.

A positive fitting record pairs the selected frozen preparation, independent alternatives, region evidence and a finished approved three-view board. Include a wrong candidate with its diagnosed fault, such as pasted iris ornaments or inconsistent rear supports. The learner gets visible supervision for the meaningful difference. Taxonomy IDs identify the concepts and retain their original meanings.
