### Review example for Character preparation

Iris contributes three upright standards and three descending falls, with directional contrast from sword-like leaves. Porcelain contributes coherent glazed surfaces, edges, controlled thickness and the contrast between glazed and matte substrate. The preparation should produce actual body-owned construction and a compelling Anime identity.

Candidate A has a tall, quiet silhouette, three porcelain-backed upright blades born at the crown and three long curved laminae descending behind an open face. Their support continues through thin shoulders and a soft glaze-to-matte transition at the elbows. The focal color is deep indigo around the eyes and the thinnest edges. Main planes stay ivory, with restrained cobalt accents. The rear view would expose the shared crown roots and the profile would show how the falling laminae clear the neck. Candidate B concentrates the upright geometry around the shoulders and gives the head shorter blade shapes with a wider, lower silhouette. Candidate A is the review preference for its face hierarchy, recognizable downward sweep and continuous support. The pair lacks final visual acceptance.

A positive fitting record pairs the selected frozen preparation, independent alternatives, region evidence and a finished approved three-view board. Include a wrong candidate with its diagnosed fault, such as pasted iris ornaments or inconsistent rear supports. The learner gets visible supervision for the meaningful difference. Taxonomy IDs identify the concepts and retain their original meanings.

Representative style-swap supervision uses one fixed target character and scene with two independently sourced style authorities. For example, contrast the finely edged, spacious treatment in 1000185023 with the soft directional light and clustered translucent highlights in 1000189804. The paired target illustrations must show both treatments on the same new subject and camera. Annotate contours, edge transitions, shadow bands, highlight groupings and detail hierarchy in the two finished images. Record the reference subjects separately from the target. These illustrations supply the treatment comparison. A matched pair with fitting authorization is still needed.

Train the initial style head using a frozen patch encoder with a trainable role query and residual projection. Apply a contrastive loss to different subjects rendered in the same treatment and matched subjects in different treatments. Within a style swap, use the fixed identity and layout readouts for preservation losses. Test unseen style groups, different characters sharing a style, and the same character across styles. Use learned technical readouts to locate failures and a blind visual comparison to judge whether the applied treatment is recognizably the requested one. In the next fitting stage train the conditioning projection and test low-rank K/V updates on the Anime receiver using flow matching and the same protected attributes. Record each source split, updated checkpoint and accepted output separately.

### Catalogue, sampling and ratings

The owner preference for the overall corpus is **25% SFW, 40% suggestive/ecchi and 35% NSFW**. For every training run, report the achieved eligible mix and the reason for its subset. Style swaps need independently labeled technique and subject diversity. Character preparation needs multi-view design evidence. Theme skills need scene and composition coverage. A rating learner needs real, explicit 0–100 scores. Preserve the differences between these tasks when selecting samples.
