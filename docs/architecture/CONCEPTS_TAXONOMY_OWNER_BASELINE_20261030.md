## Normative source rules

1. **Authority separation:** §0 makes the file a knowledge companion for Character Creation, not a source of workflow phases, candidate counts, freezes or rendering order.
2. **Four dispositions:** §§1–2 distinguish selectable SC, variant/subconcept, non-selectable materialization knowledge, and reject. Selectable admission needs premium female-character promise, immediate recognizability in neutral reference posture, material and reciprocal fusion value, multiple coherent routes, and the ability to survive the hostile siphonophore test. Abstract/process concepts have an elevated gate.
3. **Single source-backed leaf contract:** §3.10 records the complete obligations for each SC identity: ID, canonical path, aliases/views, mandatory anatomy and anchors, counts/variation, non-optional body-plan invariants, material, symbiosis, transformation range, interpretation boundaries, premium potential, and separate downstream clothing/weapon contributions. Unknown and explicit N/A are not interchangeable.
4. **One recursive selector graph:** §4 uses BC/SF containers, SC leaves, aliases, retired IDs, domain views, search facets and intersection. Aliases do not create new canonical parentage. Do not reuse retired or allocated IDs; use the next new unused SC identifier only after admission.
5. **Keep mechanisms non-selectable:** §§7/7A define MK and T records as structural tools, not independently selectable concept identities.
6. **Methodology-bound application:** the Character Creation methodology alone owns execution and any freeze/authorization contract. The taxonomy supports rather than controls it.

## Change protocol for any new taxonomy

A candidate new taxonomy must specify the precise *object being classified*, an admission gate tied to its target methodology, canonical path/membership and immutable IDs, child/parent semantics, leaf-level obligations, aliases and cross-cutting views, non-selectable mechanisms/variants, conflict resolution, and source evidence. It must pass an explicit owner approval gate **before** its first categories or entries enter the application.

The owner has approved `Concepts.md` as the baseline **architecture and rigor** for future taxonomies. The owner has **not** approved cloning the Character Creation ontology into other modes or the assistant's previously invented 14-topic dictionary and sample Theme atom categories. Those have no canonical status.
